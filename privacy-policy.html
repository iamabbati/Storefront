Privacy policy

Last updated: this store is still in production — payment processing and some sections below describe how data is handled once fully live.

What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to run your account, your cart, and your orders:

  • Account information. Your name, email address, and password are handled by Supabase Auth, our authentication provider. We never see or store your raw password — Supabase stores it hashed, and we only receive a session token proving you're signed in.
  • Order and shipping data. When you place an order, we store the shipping name, phone number, delivery address, city, and state you provide, along with the items, quantities, and total for that order, so it can be fulfilled and so you can see your order history.
  • Theme preference. Whether you've chosen light or dark mode is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only. It is never sent to our servers.
  • Cart contents. If you're signed in, your cart is saved so it's there when you come back. If you're browsing without an account, your cart exists only in memory for that visit and isn't saved anywhere.

What we don't collect

We do not store your card number, expiry date, or CVV. Payment processing on this site is not yet connected to a live payment gateway — those fields are currently placeholders and are never read, transmitted, or saved by our code, in any form.

How your data is protected

Your account data, cart, and orders are stored in a Postgres database with row-level security enabled on every table. In plain terms: the database itself enforces that you can only ever read or write your own cart items and your own orders — not anyone else's — no matter how a request reaches it.

Third parties

We use Supabase to host our database and handle authentication. We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers or other third parties.

Your choices

You can update your account details or sign out at any time from the Account page. To request that your account and associated data be deleted, contact us using the details on our Contact page.
